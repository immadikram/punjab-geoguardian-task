Today, I completed AIRI Team PITB - AI Internship Task 3, where I built an end-to-end GeoAI project using satellite imagery and machine learning.

For this task, I developed Punjab GeoGuardian, a prototype that detects possible urban expansion and farmland loss in Lahore peri-urban zone (Raiwind Road corridor) by comparing Sentinel-2 imagery from 2019 and 2025.

Key results (estimated): about 7.24 sq km (5.67% of the AOI) shows possible conversion from vegetation/agriculture to built-up land. Validation on 59 manually checked points gave ≈ 32.2% accuracy.

The project covered the complete GeoAI workflow:
- Area of Interest selection
- Satellite imagery collection
- NDVI and NDBI calculation
- Land-use classification
- Change detection
- Area estimation
- Map visualization
- Validation and error analysis
- Final documentation

Tools used: Python, Google Colab, Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, Geemap/Folium, Scikit-learn, Streamlit.

Key learning: AI becomes more powerful when it is connected with real-world geography, public data, and decision-making problems.
